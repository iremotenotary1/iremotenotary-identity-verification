Prepare before the session

Four practical checks

1

Use a valid photo ID

Have an accepted government-issued photo ID available and make sure it is readable and unexpired where required.

2

Review the name

Compare the name on the document with the name on your ID before the session begins.

3

Prepare your device

Use a reliable camera, microphone, browser, and internet connection in a well-lit location.

4

Keep documents unsigned

Unless instructed otherwise, wait to sign until you are connected with the notary.

Identity verification in context

Different signers may follow different verification paths

Domestic online-notary sessions may involve credential analysis and additional identity-proofing steps where applicable. For signers outside the United States, iRemoteNotary uses supported platforms that provide biometric identity verification rather than relying on U.S.-based knowledge questions.

Final requirements can vary by session, document, platform, and applicable law. Confirm your ID and location before the appointment.
