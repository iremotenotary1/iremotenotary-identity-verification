How the iRemoteNotary Identity & Verification Center handles information on this static microsite.

Technical information

When you visit this site, Netlify may process standard web-server and security information such as IP address, browser type, and request metadata as part of hosting and delivery.

The static source files for this microsite do not intentionally implement a first-party analytics or advertising system. iRemoteNotary does not control cookies, server logs, embedded content, data retention, or other processing that may occur through Netlify, FeedWalls, or external websites you visit from this page.

The curated resource feed is embedded from FeedWalls (feedwalls.online). When the feed loads, FeedWalls and linked publishers may receive technical information such as browser type, referrer, and interaction data under their own privacy practices.

External links

Links on this site may direct you to iRemoteNotary, NIST, the FTC, or other external websites. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies and terms. After you follow an external link, this microsite does not control cookies or tracking technologies used by those publishers.

Contact links

When you call, email, or follow a link to the main iRemoteNotary website, the information you provide is handled under the policies applicable to that service.

What this page does not do

This resource page does not sell personal information and does not request sensitive identity documents through a form on this microsite.
